About Dr. Siva Prasad

Head of Dermatology and Cosmetic Surgery at Mediclinic AlNoor Hospital, Abu Dhabi — a top-rated, JCIA-accredited institution managed by Mediclinic International, Switzerland — with over 15 years of departmental leadership.

Over 32 years of experience in dermatology, combining clinical practice and academic expertise. Specialization includes managing acne, psoriasis, vitiligo, skin cancers, birthmarks, and other complex or treatment-resistant conditions.

Active participation in national and international dermatology conferences, with contributions to research and clinical trials. Among the pioneers in introducing biologic treatments for skin diseases in the UAE.

Member of esteemed professional organizations, including the European Academy of Dermatology and Venereology.
